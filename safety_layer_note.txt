Safety Layer Note

The maintenance advisor contains an offline safety layer.

The safety layer identifies safety topics related to the detected component
and maintenance intent. It adds relevant warnings from the structured
knowledge base and manual evidence.

The safety layer works without Groq, Llama, or any other online language model.

The optional LLM is used only to improve the wording of the final answer.
It must not create new safety instructions, remove warnings, or replace the
offline advisor.

If the LLM is unavailable, the original offline answer and safety warnings
are returned.
